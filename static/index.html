Quests & Campaign Progression

MAIN CAMPAIGN

The Last Light of Duskendale

Beneath the crossroads well at the heart of Duskendale lies a sealed vault — something old that drained the magic out of the village overnight decades ago. Sir Besrand the Last sealed it with stone and iron. He is dead now, and someone has forged a key.

A thin iron circlet pulled from the sealed vault generations ago. Grants Advantage on Insight checks to detect deception and perceive hidden motives.

Currency

Old Dusken coinage minted during the reign of the High Marshals. Accepted across the realm and valued by peddlers and blacksmiths alike.

Divine Boon — 1 Use Remaining

A standing pledge from Brother Adalric and the order. Grants one free divine intervention: high-tier blessing, shelter, or complete bodily restoration.
